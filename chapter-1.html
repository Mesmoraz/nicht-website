Chapter 1 – Anger

The journey begins with red.

The Premise

You wake in a familiar place. It's drained of color — a grayscale of memory and loss. Your task is simple: search. Move through the still rooms. Hold the objects you find. And with each one, the world begins to return.

The game is about what you discover, not where you go. It's about the stillness before understanding, and the weight of seeing again.

Theme & Tone

Emotion: Anger is not rage. It's the weight of loss, the sharp edge of what should have been. It's the color red — not as violence, but as clarity. As feeling.

Atmosphere: Near-black rooms. Minimal sound. The reveal of each color is a small moment of grace. The pace is deliberate. There is no rush.

Challenge: The puzzles are about observation. They reward patience and curiosity. There are no arbitrary barriers — only doors that open when you're ready to see.

Core Gameplay

  • Exploration: Move through interconnected rooms at your own pace.
  • Interaction: Hold objects to reveal color and unlock understanding.
  • Puzzle Discovery: Solutions emerge through observation, not trial-and-error.
  • Emotional Pacing: Each room builds on the last. The story unfolds through environment.
