About Nicht

Nicht is a moody first-person puzzle adventure about discovering color and emotion in a world stripped of both. It launches with Chapter 1 – Anger, a complete 1–2 hour experience exploring loss, clarity, and the weight of seeing again.

Genre: First-person puzzle adventure with narrative elements
Platform: Windows PC (Steam)
Release Target: 2026 (TBA)
Price: $2.99 USD

About the Studio

Nicht is developed by an independent creative team focused on emotional, color-driven game experiences. The studio prioritizes atmospheric design, deliberate pacing, and meaningful player engagement.

Key Facts

  • Chapter 1 – Anger is the first complete emotional arc in a planned six-chapter experience
  • Core mechanic: holding objects to reveal color and unlock understanding
  • Puzzle design emphasizes observation and curiosity over trial-and-error
  • Visual identity: near-black base with restrained anger-red accents; subtle film-grain texture
  • Inspired by reflective mystery games, but with a distinct focus on color and emotion
