Chapter 1 – Anger

The journey begins with red.

Chapter 1 – Anger is a complete emotional experience. You wake in a familiar place, stripped of color. Piece by piece, you'll search for what's been lost. The mood is heavy. The puzzles are deliberate. The reveal is profound.
