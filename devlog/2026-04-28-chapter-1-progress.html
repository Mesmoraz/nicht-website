Chapter 1 Room Design Pass

April 28, 2026

This week marked a significant milestone in Chapter 1 development. We've completed the spatial layout and atmospheric design for all six Chapter 1 rooms. Here's what we've accomplished.

Spatial Design

Each room has been carefully crafted to guide exploration without feeling linear. They're interconnected in a way that encourages backtracking and discovery. The architecture supports the core interaction mechanic: holding objects to reveal color.

  • All 6 Chapter 1 rooms fully modeled and navigable
  • Each room has 3–5 interaction points for color discovery
  • Navigation flow tested and refined

Color Palette & Atmosphere

The visual identity has been locked. The base world is near-black (desaturated, heavy), and the accent color is anger-red — not aggressive, but clear and emotional.

  • Base palette: #0a0a0a (near-black) with subtle gray mid-tones
  • Accent color: #c41e3a (anger-red) — used sparingly for narrative impact
  • Texture: subtle film-grain applied globally for a vintage, contemplative feel

Lighting Progress

The lighting pass is well underway. We're using minimal, motivated light sources to maintain mood and focus player attention. Each room's lighting subtly guides exploration.

  • Primary light per room (motivated by in-world source)
  • Ambient occlusion to enhance depth and atmosphere
  • Bloom on color reveals to create subtle visual impact

What's Next

Next week we'll begin detailed audio implementation and continue refining the puzzle logic. The goal is to have a fully playable vertical slice by mid-May.
